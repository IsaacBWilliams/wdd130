Popular Trip Options

Rental Trip

The Rental Trip is perfect for adventurers who want to enjoy the river on their own. Rent the equipment you need and choose your own route, pace, and adventure. It’s a simple and flexible way to experience the excitement of white water rafting!

Relaxing Trip

The Guided Relaxation Trip is perfect for those who want to enjoy the river at a calm and comfortable pace. Our friendly guides will lead the way while you take in the beautiful scenery and enjoy a relaxing rafting experience.

Crazy Trip

The Crazy Trip is made for adventurers looking for an unforgettable thrill! Our experienced guides will lead you through intense rapids, wild turns, and exciting challenges. Get ready for an extreme rafting experience packed with action from start to finish!
